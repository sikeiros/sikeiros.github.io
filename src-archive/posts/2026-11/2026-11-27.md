While my visit to Tbilisi, I was asked at a very last moment to substitute a teacher at Typomania lettering workshop, which I almost immediately said yes to. I had a day to prepare the things I wanted to share with students and to immerse into a given plan. Students would walk everyday on the streets of Tbilisi, and in the evening they'd come to the studio space all inspired by their street findings, and work with a given medium. Unfortunately, I didn't join them for walks, since merely days before that I fell in a pit (literal) and my mobility was reduced greatly. I met them in the studio space and discussed what they saw earier and what would they like to explore while drawing letters. But I think I nailed it, more importantly, students absolutely nailed it!

In the meantime, the research about the first Georgian type, that I conducted with my friend Yuri, began to bloom in different shapes. 

I gave four talks in total, and wish to do more (wink-wink). I wrote a publication with our findings and constructed narrative, the publication was edited by Adelina Shaidullina, designed by Daria Yarzhambek and Yuri Ostromentsky, and printed in Latvia thanks to our friend Alexey Murashko – after that, spreaded through three different countries, so drop me a message if you want a copy (wink-wink). 

With that, we were supposed to have the exhibition in BULAC, my favourite library in Paris, unfortunately because of the political situation the library remains closed and the exhibition is closed to a public for the time being. We were allowed to see it as a "commissariat", so I give you some photos.
